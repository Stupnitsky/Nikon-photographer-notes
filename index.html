The S-Line difference.

The S-Line designation is reserved for premium NIKKOR Z lenses — 24 of the 49 in the table. It denotes tighter optical tolerances, dust- and drip-resistant construction, a control ring with display or L-Fn button, and quieter autofocus for video. It does not denote a faster maximum aperture: the 35mm f/1.4 lists at $579.95, the S-Line 35mm f/1.8 at $749.95.

Two f/2.8 zoom trios

Same coverage, half the price.

The professional S-Line trio — 14-24mm, 24-70mm and 70-200mm — and the compact trio of 17-28mm, 28-75mm and 70-180mm both run from wide angle to short telephoto at a constant f/2.8. The difference is weight, build and cost.
